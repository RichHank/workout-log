# About The Standards

The standards screen is a lightweight comparison tool, not coaching advice or a medical recommendation.

The app bundles a small starter strength-standard model for these lifts:

- Bench Press
- Squat
- Deadlift
- Overhead Press
- Barbell Row
- Pull-up

The training-level labels follow the common strength-standard bands used by ExRx: untrained, novice, intermediate, advanced, and elite. ExRx describes those bands as adult one-rep max performance standards based on historical powerlifting and weightlifting classifications.

The public benchmark idea is also informed by Strength Level, which publishes community strength standards based on logged lifts by sex, bodyweight, and exercise.

Sources:

- https://exrx.net/Testing/WeightLifting/StrengthStandards
- https://exrx.net/Testing/WeightLifting/BenchStandards
- https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards

The bundled values are intentionally coarse, rounded, and app-side. For official standards, use the source websites directly.
